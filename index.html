Bay Bridge Crossing

Drive the full 4.3-mile William Preston Lane Jr. Memorial Bridge — Sandy Point to Stevensville. Up over the western suspension span, through the steel truss cage above the eastern channel, then the long trestle to Kent Island. End to end is about four minutes.
